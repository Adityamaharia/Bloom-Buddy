Mental Health Tips

😌 Stress Management

Take Deep Breaths: Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 4. Repeat 5 times.

Take Breaks: Every hour, step away from your screen for 5 minutes.

Move Your Body: A 10-minute walk can significantly reduce stress.

Limit Caffeine: Too much caffeine can increase anxiety.

😴 Better Sleep

Consistent Schedule: Go to bed and wake up at the same time daily.

Dark Environment: Keep your room cool, dark, and quiet.

No Screens Before Bed: Avoid screens 30 minutes before sleep.

Relaxation Techniques: Try meditation or progressive muscle relaxation.

🧘 Mindfulness & Meditation

5-Minute Meditation: Focus on your breath. Let thoughts pass without judgment.

Body Scan: Close your eyes. Notice sensations from your head to toes.

Mindful Walking: Pay attention to each step, breath, and surroundings.

Journaling: Write your thoughts without judgment. Clarity often follows.

📅 Daily Habits for Well-Being

Morning Gratitude: List 3 things you're grateful for each morning.

Hydration: Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily.

Sunlight: Get 15-20 minutes of sunlight in the morning.

Social Connection: Spend time with loved ones or have a meaningful conversation.

💚 Remember: Your mental health matters. Be kind to yourself.
